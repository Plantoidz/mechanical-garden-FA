### Oceanic Facts for My Reference:

- **Marine Protected Areas (MPAs):** Globally, MPAs cover about 8% of the ocean, significantly short of the international goal of protecting at least 30% by 2030.
- **Plankton:** Phytoplankton produce approximately 50% of Earth's oxygen and form the base of most marine food webs.
- **Krill:** Antarctic krill populations have declined by around 80% since the 1970s due to climate change and overfishing, significantly impacting marine wildlife dependent on krill.
- **Coral Reefs:** Approximately 50% of coral reefs have been lost in recent decades due to warming oceans, pollution, and acidification.
- **Plastic Pollution:** About 8 million tons of plastic enter the ocean annually, with marine life often mistaking plastic for food, causing injury or death.
- **Overfishing:** Roughly one-third of the world’s fisheries are overexploited, leading to declining fish populations and disrupted marine ecosystems.
